Your AI Stack Is the Problem, Not the AI

Ask most people how many AI tools they pay for and they will start counting on their fingers. A chat assistant. An image generator. A transcription tool. Something for slides. Something for research. Something a colleague swore by in March.

Each one works. Together they cost you the thing you were trying to save: focus.

The hidden tax of context switching

Every time you move a task between tools, you pay twice. Once to re-explain the context, and once to re-read what you produced before.

Do that six times a day and you have lost an hour to nothing at all.

Worse, the work fragments. The research lives in one tab, the draft in another, the image somewhere else, and the final deck in a fourth. Nobody — including you — can see the whole thread anymore.

What "one workspace" actually means

A single workspace is not a bundle discount. It is a different working model:

  • The research, the images, the documents and the browsing share one context.
  • You describe the outcome once.
  • The agent keeps the thread, so the fifth step still knows what the first step found.

That last point is the whole game. Context is the scarce resource, not compute.

A simple audit

Open your browser and count the tabs related to one project. Then count how many times today you copied text from one tool into another.

That number is your real AI cost.
